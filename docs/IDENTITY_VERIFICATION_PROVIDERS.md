# Identity Verification Providers for Mexico — Research & Comparison Report

**Prepared for:** Velo (Stellar-based cash exchange platform, Mexico)  
**Date:** July 2026  
**Scope:** Evaluation of lightweight identity verification (KYC) and SMS confirmation providers for onboarding peer-to-peer cash providers (liquidity providers) in Mexico at early-stage/small scale.  

---

## Executive Summary

For an early-stage, cost-sensitive deployment onboarding cash providers in Mexico, standard enterprise KYC suites (which charge $150–$500+/month minimum commitments) present an unnecessary financial bottleneck. 

To maintain regulatory alignment under Mexico's Anti-Money Laundering framework (**LFPIORPI**) and ensure secure peer-to-peer transactions without high fixed overheads, Velo should adopt a **Tiered Onboarding Strategy**:

1. **Tier 0 (Phone Possession / Low-Value Onboarding):** **Firebase Phone Authentication** (Free tier up to 10,000 verifications/month) or **SMS Masivos** ($0.23–$0.90 MXN per SMS / ~$0.012–$0.047 USD).
2. **Tier 1 (Lightweight Document & Database Verification — Recommended Primary):** **Didit** (Free tier of 500 Full KYC bundles/month, $0.20 USD per CURP / INE query, $0.33 USD per Full KYC check thereafter with no monthly minimums).
3. **Tier 2 (High-Volume / Formal Regulatory Escalation):** **Sumsub** (Starts at $1.35 USD/check with $149 USD/month minimum commitment) or **MetaMap / Incode** (Sales-led enterprise contract) when verification volume exceeds 1,000+ providers/month or formal CNBV/SAT reporting mandates full audit logging.

**Estimated Cost per Verification for Recommended Primary Stack (Didit + Local SMS):**  
- **0.00 USD to $0.35 USD per verified cash provider** at early stage (<500 providers/mo).

## 1. Overview of Identity Verification Needs for Velo in Mexico

Under Mexican law, peer-to-peer cryptocurrency/fiat exchange services touch LFPIORPI regulations regarding "Vulnerable Activities." When onboarding cash providers (who manage physical cash liquidity), Velo must manage two primary verification layers:

1. **SMS Confirmation (Phone Verification):** Confirms contactability, mobile number ownership, and prevents automated spam/sybil accounts.
2. **Official Document & Database Checks:**
   - **INE (Instituto Nacional Electoral):** The primary official photo voter ID in Mexico. Verification checks document validity (vigencia), key credentials (CIC, OCR), and prevents forged cards.
   - **CURP (Clave Única de Registro de Población):** The unique national identity code. Validation queries the **RENAPO** (Registro Nacional de Población) central database.
   - **Biometric / Liveness:** Selfie matching against the INE photo to prevent identity impersonation.
   - **RFC (Registro Federal de Contribuyentes):** Tax identifier validation with SAT (optional/Tier 2 for professional operators).

## 2. Detailed Provider Analysis

### 2.1 SMS Confirmation-Based Verification

#### A. Firebase Phone Authentication
* **What it verifies:** Phone number possession via 6-digit SMS OTP.
* **Pricing Model:** Publicly confirmed. Free tier includes up to **10,000 SMS verifications/month**. Subsequent usage is ~$0.01–$0.06 USD per SMS depending on carrier.
* **Integration Complexity:** Low (Firebase Client SDK for Web/Mobile & Admin SDK for server verification).
* **Mexico Specifics:** Uses global aggregator routing. SMS delivery to Mexican carriers (Telcel, AT&T, Movistar, Altán) is generally high, but international routing can occasionally experience latency or carrier filtering.
* **Verdict:** Excellent starting choice for Tier 0 phone verification given the generous free tier.

#### B. SMS Masivos (smsmasivos.com.mx)
* **What it verifies:** Phone number possession via custom SMS OTP generated by Velo backend.
* **Pricing Model:** Publicly confirmed. Volume-based prepaid credit in MXN.  
  - $0.90 MXN (~$0.047 USD) per SMS at low volume (500 credits).  
  - $0.26–$0.47 MXN (~$0.013–$0.025 USD) per SMS at higher volume.  
  - **$0 monthly minimum fee**, no expiration on purchased credits.
* **Integration Complexity:** Medium (REST API integration required to send OTP code and manage verification state).
* **Mexico Specifics:** 100% Mexico-focused with direct carrier routes (Telcel, AT&T, Movistar, Altán Redes). Provides local CFDI 4.0 tax invoices. 98–99% delivery success rate.
* **Verdict:** Best fallback or primary SMS provider if global routing (Firebase/Twilio) encounters deliverability issues in Mexico.

#### C. Twilio Verify
* **What it verifies:** Phone number possession via SMS, WhatsApp, or Voice OTP.
* **Pricing Model:** Publicly confirmed.  
  - Base fee: **$0.05 USD** per successful verification.  
  - Plus carrier SMS fee: **$0.1819 USD** per outbound SMS to Mexico.  
  - **Total cost per verification:** **~$0.2319 USD** (~$4.60 MXN).
* **Integration Complexity:** Low (Twilio SDK & Verify API endpoints).
* **Mexico Specifics:** High cost relative to local channels. Requires pre-registration for alphanumeric sender IDs to avoid filtering.
* **Verdict:** Expensive for early-stage Mexico deployment (~5–10x higher cost than local SMS or Firebase). Recommended only if multi-channel (WhatsApp + SMS) fallback orchestration is mandatory.

### 2.2 Document Verification & KYC Services

#### A. Didit (didit.me)
* **What it verifies:** Mexican INE validity (`mex_ine_vigencia`), CURP verification against RENAPO database (`mex_curp`), Proof of Address (`mex_residential`), full ID document OCR, 3D passive liveness, face matching.
* **Pricing Model:** Publicly confirmed.  
  - **Free Tier:** **500 free Full KYC bundles every month, forever** (includes ID verification + liveness + face match + IP/device check).  
  - **Database Queries:** **$0.20 USD** per successful CURP check; **$0.20 USD** per successful INE validity check.  
  - **Paid KYC Volume:** **$0.33 USD** per Full KYC check after exceeding the 500 free monthly bundle threshold.  
  - **$0 monthly minimum fee**, pay-as-you-go.
* **Integration Complexity:** Low–Medium (API-first, pre-built Web/Mobile SDK widget, no-code workflow builder).
* **Mexico Specifics:** Direct native endpoints for Mexican government database lookups (RENAPO & INE).
* **Verdict:** **Top Recommendation for early-stage Velo deployment.** The 500 free monthly checks and zero monthly commitment eliminate upfront risk.

#### B. Sumsub (sumsub.com)
* **What it verifies:** Global & Mexican government IDs (INE, Passport, Driver's License), CURP validation, 3D Liveness, face match, AML watchlist/PEP screening, proof of address.
* **Pricing Model:** Publicly confirmed base plan.  
  - **Basic Plan:** **$1.35 USD** per verification attempt, subject to a **$149 USD monthly minimum commitment**. Includes 14-day free trial (50 free checks).  
  - **Compliance Plan:** **$1.85 USD** per verification with a **$299 USD monthly minimum commitment** (adds ongoing AML monitoring & proof of address).
* **Integration Complexity:** Medium (Robust Web/Mobile SDKs, Webhooks, workflow orchestration builder).
* **Mexico Specifics:** Full compliance suite tailored to CNBV & LFPIORPI requirements with exportable audit logs.
* **Verdict:** Best option once Velo reaches 500+ active cash providers monthly and requires automated ongoing AML screening.

#### C. MetaMap / Incode (metamap.com / incode.com)
* **What it verifies:** Mexican INE validity, CURP (RENAPO), RFC (SAT), biometric face match, liveness detection, proof of address.
* **Pricing Model:** **Sales-Led / Estimated.**  
  - No public per-check pricing published on website.  
  - *Estimated Range:* **$0.80 – $1.50 USD per verification** based on industry benchmarks for mid-volume LATAM contracts. Typically requires annual commit or volume minimums.
* **Integration Complexity:** Low–Medium (Drop-in SDK widget for mobile/web).
* **Mexico Specifics:** Industry leader in LATAM with deep, real-time Mexican government database ties.
* **Verdict:** Excellent enterprise solution, but sales-led pricing and potential minimums make it less suitable for initial low-volume testing.

#### D. Truora (truora.com)
* **What it verifies:** Mexican digital identity, criminal & background checks, INE verification, WhatsApp-native conversational KYC.
* **Pricing Model:** **Sales-Led / Estimated.**  
  - Entry-level platform plans report starting at **~$83 USD/month** base.  
  - *Estimated Per-Check Cost:* **$0.50 – $1.20 USD per check** depending on module (background check vs. identity).
* **Integration Complexity:** Medium (REST API & WhatsApp bot integration).
* **Mexico Specifics:** Strong LATAM focus; specializes in low-friction WhatsApp onboarding (popular in Mexico).
* **Verdict:** High-potential candidate if Velo decides to onboard cash providers entirely through WhatsApp rather than a web/mobile app.

#### E. TuIdentidad & Nubarium (tuidentidad.com / nubarium.com)
* **What it verifies:** Official Mexican INE credential validation, CURP (RENAPO) verification, RFC (SAT) tax status query, facial recognition.
* **Pricing Model:** **Sales-Led / Estimated.**  
  - Enterprise quote-based pricing.  
  - *Estimated Range:* **$0.30 – $1.00 USD per query** depending on package and direct database API usage.
* **Integration Complexity:** API-based.
* **Mexico Specifics:** 100% focused on Mexican legal/regulatory compliance (CNBV, SAT).
* **Verdict:** Strong alternative for pure Mexican database queries if direct government verification without international SDK overhead is desired.

#### F. Persona (withpersona.com)
* **What it verifies:** Government ID (Mexican INE, passport), selfie liveness, device fingerprinting, AML screening.
* **Pricing Model:** Publicly confirmed starter tier / Estimated growth plans.  
  - **Starter Tier:** **500 free ID verifications/month** (government ID + selfie check).  
  - **Growth Tier:** Custom pricing (sources estimate starting around $250 USD/month).
* **Integration Complexity:** Low (Hosted flow, SDKs).
* **Mexico Specifics:** Supports Mexican INE OCR and liveness, but lacks native direct RENAPO/INE database query integrations on lower tiers.
* **Verdict:** Strong runner-up for international ID checking due to free tier, but lacks native Mexican database lookup depth compared to Didit.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations for Velo

### 4.1 Recommended Stack for Small-Scale Launch (<500 Cash Providers/Month)

For low-volume, early-stage deployment, Velo should combine **Firebase Phone Auth** with **Didit**:

1. **Step 1: Phone Verification (Tier 0)**  
   - Implement **Firebase Phone Authentication** for mobile OTP during initial registration.  
   - *Cost:* **$0.00 USD** (within 10,000 free monthly verification allowance).

2. **Step 2: Cash Provider Identity Verification (Tier 1)**  
   - Integrate **Didit** Web/Mobile SDK for cash provider onboarding.  
   - Collect and verify: Mexican INE photo ID + Passive 3D Liveness + CURP database lookup (`mex_curp`).  
   - *Cost:*  
     - Verifications 1 to 500 per month: **$0.00 USD** (under Didit's free monthly bundle allowance).  
     - Database query add-ons (if mandatory CURP verification is run separately): **$0.20 USD** per check.  
   - *Result:* Total cost per onboarded cash provider is **$0.00 to $0.20 USD**.

3. **Step 3: Failover & Scale Plan (Tier 2 Upgrade Trigger)**  
   - If international SMS delivery to Mexican carriers experiences latency, switch SMS OTP fallback to **SMS Masivos** ($0.26 MXN / ~$0.013 USD per message).  
   - When active cash provider onboarding consistently exceeds 500 verifications/month or requires automated daily PEP/AML watchlist screening for CNBV audit compliance, migrate to **Sumsub** ($149/month plan).

## 6. Regulatory & Technical Considerations for Mexico

1. **INE Validity Verification (`vigencia`):** In Mexico, voter IDs expire periodically, and invalidated cards are frequently used in fraud attempt spikes. Verifying the INE's active status against the official electoral registry via API is critical before approving cash liquidity providers.
2. **Biometric CURP Compliance:** Mexico is transitioning toward mandatory biometric CURP registration. Providers like Didit, MetaMap, and TuIdentidad query RENAPO to validate CURP status in real time.
3. **Data Protection (LFPDPPP):** Personal identity data collected in Mexico must comply with the *Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares*. Using established SDK providers (Didit/Sumsub/MetaMap) ensures encrypted transmission and data retention compliance.
